Choose up to three videos, one per camera, and upload them together as one session. Each video gets the OpenCV 5 front end's quality analysis: tracking, features and blur over the whole clip, with weak stretches marked by the coordinator's rule. A single video has no stereo partner or calibration, so the videos are not merged by Swarm-SLAM. Max 95 MB per video; uploading needs the operator token.
